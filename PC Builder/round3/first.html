Round 1: PC Assembly (Drag & Drop) (50 seconds)

  1. A virtual PC image will be shown with empty slots.
  2. Drag and drop the correct components (CPU, RAM, GPU, Storage, PSU, etc.) to the right place.
  3. You have 50 seconds to complete this task.
  4. Bonus points for completing all placements correctly before time ends.
  5. Submit before the timer ends to secure your score.

Round 2: Matching Budget (50 seconds)

  1. You will be given a fixed budget (For example (₹1,00,000)).
  2. Select compatible components for either Normal, Gaming, or Workstation builds.
  3. Stay within the budget and ensure performance + compatibility.
  4. You have 50 seconds to finalize your build.
  5. Overspending beyond the budget will not be considered valid.
  6. Scoring is based on compatibility, performance fit, and cost efficiency.

Round 3: Troubleshooting (90 seconds)

  1. You will be given 3 problem scenarios (boot errors, overheating, no display, lag, etc.).
  2. For each scenario, choose the correct solution from the options provided.
  3. After choosing, explain why you selected that answer and justify it clearly.
  4. You have 90 seconds total for all scenarios.
  5. Scores are based on accuracy, explanation clarity, and correct troubleshooting steps.
  6. The judges’ decision will be final.
